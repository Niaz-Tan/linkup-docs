🏠 Guide 📋 Roles

📋 Roles

All roles in the server fall into one of four categories. Each category serves a different purpose, whether it's tracking your study progress, showing when you joined, identifying your community, or helping manage the server.

📈 Ranking Roles

Role Explanation
🌱 Member The starting rank for every member. Earn more study hours to climb the leaderboard.
🥉 Bronze I Awarded after reaching 5 study hours. Your study journey officially begins here.
🥉 Bronze II Earned between 15–30 study hours, showing growing consistency.
🥉 Bronze III Given to members with 30–50 study hours.
🥈 Silver I Awarded after reaching 50 study hours. A sign of regular dedication.
🥈 Silver II Earned between 70–95 study hours.
🥈 Silver III Given to members with 95–125 study hours.
🥇 Gold I Awarded after reaching 125 study hours.
🥇 Gold II Earned between 160–200 study hours.
🥇 Gold III Given to members with 200–250 study hours.
💠 Platinum I Awarded after reaching 250 study hours.
💠 Platinum II Earned between 300–360 study hours.
💠 Platinum III Given to members with 360–420 study hours.
💎 Diamond I Awarded after reaching 420 study hours.
💎 Diamond II Earned between 480–540 study hours.
💎 Diamond III Given to members with 540–600 study hours.
👑 VIP — Celestial Scholar The highest study rank, awarded to members with 600+ study hours. Represents exceptional consistency and dedication.
📌 Note: Ranking roles are based on study hours earned during the current season and are reset at the beginning of every new season.

🏆 Season Roles

Role Explanation
🌱 Pre-Season Joined the community before the official launch of Season 1. These members helped shape the server during its early days.
🏆 Season 1 Joined during the first official season.
🏆 Season 2 Joined during the second official season.
🏆 Season 3 Joined during the third official season.
🏆 Future Seasons New season roles will be introduced every six months as the community grows.
📌 Note: Season roles are permanent and never reset. They simply show when you first joined the community.

🛡️ Management Roles

Role Explanation
👑 Owner The creator and primary manager of the server. Responsible for the overall vision, development, and major decisions.
🛠️ Admin Helps manage the server, configure systems, organize events, and oversee moderation.
🛡️ Moderator Maintains a safe and welcoming environment by enforcing rules and assisting members.

🌍 Community Roles

Role Explanation
🗣️ Linguistic Indicates the languages a member can communicate in, making it easier to find people who speak the same language.
🎓 From IIUC Identifies members who study at the International Islamic University Chittagong (IIUC).
🎓 From BRAC Identifies members who study at BRAC University.
➕ More Community Roles Additional university, language, and community roles will be added as the server continues to grow.
