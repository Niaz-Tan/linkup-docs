📋 Roles

All roles in the server fall into one of four categories. Each category serves a different purpose, whether it's tracking your study progress, showing when you joined, identifying your community, or helping manage the server.

📈 Ranking Roles

Role Explanation
🌱 Member The starting rank for every member, awarded from 0–5 study hours.
🥉 Bronze I Awarded after reaching 5 study hours, covering 5–15 hours.
🥉 Bronze II Earned between 15–30 study hours, showing growing consistency.
🥉 Bronze III Given to members with 30–50 study hours.
🥈 Silver I Awarded after reaching 50 study hours, covering 50–75 hours.
🥈 Silver II Earned between 75–105 study hours.
🥈 Silver III Given to members with 105–140 study hours.
🥇 Gold I Awarded after reaching 140 study hours, covering 140–180 hours.
🥇 Gold II Earned between 180–220 study hours.
🥇 Gold III Given to members with 220–250 study hours.
💠 Platinum I Awarded after reaching 250 study hours, covering 250–270 hours.
💠 Platinum II Earned between 270–285 study hours.
💠 Platinum III Given to members with 285–295 study hours.
💎 Diamond I Awarded after reaching 295 study hours, covering 295–300 hours.
💎 Diamond II Earned between 300–305 study hours.
💎 Diamond III Given to members with 305–310 study hours.
👑 VIP — Celestial Scholar The highest study rank, awarded to members with 310+ study hours. Represents exceptional consistency and dedication.
📌 Note: Ranking roles are based on study hours earned during the current season. Each season lasts for 2 months, and all ranking progress is reset at the beginning of every new season.

🏆 Season Roles

Role Explanation
🌱 Pre-Season Joined the community before the official launch of Season 1. These members helped shape the server during its early days.
🏆 Season 1 Joined during the first official season.
🏆 Season 2 Joined during the second official season.
🏆 Season 3 Joined during the third official season.
🏆 Future Seasons New season roles will be introduced every six months as the community grows.
📌 Note: Season roles are permanent and never reset. They simply show when you first joined the community.

🌍 Community Roles

💻 Developer A role for members who work together on projects within the server. Their work and projects are visible to the community, but joining the developer group requires approval from a Server Moderator, Admin, or Owner.
🎮 Gamer A role for members who play games together. Gamers have access to a private category dedicated to gaming. To join, contact a Server Moderator, Admin, Owner, or an existing member with the Gamer role.
🐐 GOAT — Community Legend The highest honorary recognition in the community, awarded to a member whose exceptional contributions have made a lasting impact on LinkUp. This role is unique and reserved for truly extraordinary contributions.
🐣 Youngblood A unique community role reserved for the youngest member of LinkUp, recognizing their place as the next generation of the community.
🗣️ Linguist A special legacy role reserved for members who previously earned it. This role is no longer obtainable.

🛡️ Management Roles

Role Explanation
👑 Owner The creator and primary manager of the server. Responsible for the overall vision, development, and major decisions.
🛠️ Admin Helps manage the server, configure systems, organize events, and oversee moderation.
🛡️ Moderator Maintains a safe and welcoming environment by enforcing rules and assisting members.
